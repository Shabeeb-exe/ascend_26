ACADEMIC HERITAGE SINCE 1968 & 1982

The DCMS Family

Rooted in over four decades of management scholarship at the School of Business Studies, University of Calicut.

Established 1982 • AICTE Accredited DCMS

Department of Commerce & Management Studies

Established in 1982, DCMS initially functioned as a combined department and stood among the very first University Departments in Kerala to pioneer formal Management education. The same year marked the launch of its flagship full-time MBA program, accredited by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), alongside prestigious M.Com and doctoral research programs under the School of Business Studies.

DCMS houses an accomplished talent pool of senior professors, research scholars, corporate placement cells, and entrepreneurship hubs that equally champion academic rigor and national-level leadership platforms like ASCEND.

Established 1968 • "Nirmaya Karmana Sree" UoC

University of Calicut

Established in 1968, the University of Calicut (UoC) is the largest university in Kerala in terms of geographical expanse, affiliated institutions, and degrees conferred. As the second university established in Kerala, it spearheads higher education across the Malabar region and beyond.

Governed by the Calicut University Act 1975, UoC has grown into Kerala's premier residential-cum-affiliating university encompassing 34 postgraduate departments and 406 affiliated colleges.
