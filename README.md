# Ascend '26 — Official Web Portal

Official website for **Ascend '26**, the flagship national-level management festival organized annually by the **Department of Commerce and Management Studies (DCMS), University of Calicut, Kerala**.

Ascend brings together aspiring leaders and innovators from across the country to compete in high-stakes business competitions celebrating creativity, leadership, innovation, and networking.

## Tech Stack
* **Markup:** HTML5 (`index.html`)
* **Styling:** Custom CSS3 (`css/styles.css`)
* **Logic & Data:** Modular Vanilla JavaScript (`js/app.js`, `js/data.js`)
